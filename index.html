Projects

  • Flagship

    BioSynCare

    An app of carefully composed sessions — rhythmic sound, guided breathing and sensory design — for focus, relaxation, meditation and sleep preparation. It approaches sensory stimulation as non-invasive neuromodulation for health, well-being and performance.

    Formerly Audiovisual Medicine.

    biosyncare.com

Open science

Shared, citable knowledge about sensory stimulation, open to everyone.

  • SSTIM

    The Sensory Stimulation Vocabulary: an open, evolving reference that connects research, technology and AI through citable identifiers, precise descriptions and transparent evidence. It is developed through the W3C Sensory Stimulation Vocabulary Community Group, and BioSynCare adopts it and contributes to it.

    w3c-cg.github.io/sstim

  • SSTIM Workbench

    SSTIM's open reference app, formerly BSC Lab. Build sensory-stimulation patches in Patch Studio and examine the knowledge behind them, right in the browser — no account needed.

    w3c-cg.github.io/sstim/about
